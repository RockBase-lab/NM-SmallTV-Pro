## Choose what appears

Use **General → Mode**, then **Save settings**. A single app stays selected; Carousel rotates only checked apps; Timer follows up to four daily entries in the timezone selected in Display. Timer needs synchronized time and at least one usable entry.

Carousel's dwell is entered in minutes: `0.5` means 30 seconds. Stocks can rotate symbols, Pictures can rotate images and Weather can rotate clock layouts independently. A short app dwell may end before an internal rotation finishes.

## Touch control

NM-TV-154 defaults to short touch **Next page**, long touch about 0.6 seconds **Home**. Reassign these in Display. Next advances apps, including when Pictures is visible. In Carousel it follows the checked list; in Timer it previews an app for 15 seconds before resuming. Home restores the saved app, first Carousel entry or current Timer entry.

## Save and maintain

The global Save settings button saves pending edits across tabs. Show picture, Use theme and weather layout shortcuts also switch the displayed app. Export a settings backup before major changes, and keep picture/theme source packages separately.
